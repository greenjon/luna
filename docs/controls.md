# Controls

Open **Settings → Controls** to configure buttons and touch input. The available
options depend on the device.

- **Home button gestures** opens the existing gesture assignments.
- **Power button** contains **Short click** and, on X4 Pro, **Double click**.
- **Front buttons** contains remapping, orientation, Back behavior, and the two
  long-press settings at the bottom.
- **Side buttons** contains the reader layout, orientation, and page-turn long press.
- **Taps and gestures** holds every reader touch setting:
  - **Touch controls while reading** switches reader touch on or off.
  - **Tap to turn pages** and **Swipe to turn pages** choose which gestures
    turn the page.
  - **Tap zones** chooses which parts of the page turn back, turn forward, or
    open the reader menu (see below).
  - **Slide left edge for brightness** (devices with a frontlight): slide a
    finger up or down the left edge of the page to change the brightness
    while reading. A tap on the edge still turns the page.
  - **Swipe up** and **Swipe down**: an action for a vertical swipe in the
    middle of the page, such as the reader menu, Contents, Bookmark here,
    Night mode or Frontlight. Off by default. Swipes that start near the top
    or bottom edge keep their usual meaning.
  - **Show Reader Menu** (X4 Pro) chooses how the reader menu opens: Tap,
    Swipe up from the bottom edge, or Off.
  - **Reader Menu Style** chooses the List menu or the Toolbar. On devices
    without touch it stays under Settings → Reader.
  - **Toolbar buttons** chooses up to five buttons for the toolbar, left to
    right: the Contents, Text and More panels, or a direct action such as
    Bookmark, Go to %, Look up, Night mode, Frontlight, Orientation, Auto
    turn, Screenshot, Sync or Home. Empty slots are left out. Buttons for
    actions the book can't offer right now (no dictionary, no sync account)
    are hidden.

**Tap zones** draws each layout as a small page: gray turns back, white turns
forward, black opens the reader menu. Tap a layout to choose it. **Classic** is
the default: the left third turns back, the center box opens the menu, and the
rest turns forward. Classic, Three columns and the left/right halves also come
in a "back on right" version for reading with the other hand. Right-to-left
books mirror the layout. When the reader menu does not open by tap, its zone
turns forward instead.

The web settings page still lists the older **Next Page Gesture** and **Prev
Page Gesture** settings, which can set each direction separately.

**Page turn long press** is shared by Front buttons and Side buttons. Changing it
in either group changes the same setting.

On X4 Pro, **Double click** offers the same actions as **Short click**, plus
**Frontlight** to toggle the light. With a double-click action enabled, a single
click waits up to half a second before its action runs. A double click runs only
its assigned action. Set Double click to **Ignore** to remove this wait.
Reader actions such as page turn and footnotes apply while reading. Sleep,
screen refresh, Confirm, and the frontlight retain their usual behavior.
